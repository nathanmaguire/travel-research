A$2,000 a day buys an interim CAIO, not a consultant

No measured Sydney contract rate for an AI consultant, transformation lead or governance specialist reaches A$2,000. The only measured band that contains it is interim Chief AI Officer work, at A$1,908 to A$2,582.

Why it matters

Recruiter datasets put senior AI transformation consultants at A$1,016 a day (Sydney median) and AI governance leads at A$1,030 to A$1,393. The vendor price guides you used top out between A$2,200 and A$3,500. A$2,000 holds only if buyers see you as executive-level AI leadership and not as a contractor filling a seat.

The bottom line

Keep A$2,000 for strategy and fractional leadership work, sold direct and scoped around outcomes. Price governance-only work at A$1,400 to A$1,600. Do not sell engineering by the day. Two of your four baseline sources could not be confirmed as stated; see the baseline check.

By category

Six categories, three price levels

The evidence splits AI work into three price levels. Engineering, governance and transformation delivery cluster at A$800 to A$1,600. Interim leadership runs A$1,300 to A$2,600. Advisory price guides run A$1,000 to A$3,500, but nobody has measured what solo advisers actually get paid.

The bottom line

A$2,000 a day is a leadership price. Hold it for strategy and fractional CAIO work, cut it for governance-only work, and stop quoting days for build work.

The gap between measured and published rates is not noise. Contract rates price a person doing defined work in someone else's structure. Advisory rates price judgement, accountability and the absence of an agency margin. Buyers will compare you to whichever market your proposal resembles.

Method and caveats

How this was built

We started from your four sources, then searched for Australian AI rate data in six service categories. We kept only figures with a named source and a number. We tagged each one by evidence type: Measured means a recruiter dataset, salary guide or advertised role. Published means a consultancy or marketplace price guide. Derived means our own stated calculation from a published figure.

Limits that change the reading

Measured is not achieved. Galileo's 24,024-point dataset is the largest source here (snapshot 23 July 2026). But it covers advertised roles and large Sydney companies, and its leadership day rates price interim and fractional engagements, not a standing contract market.

Bases differ. Clicks quotes rates paid to the contractor, including super and excluding agency margin. Re:Sourced and most guides exclude GST. Aivy's AusTender figures include GST and cover whole teams. Treat gaps under about 10% as noise.

Some figures are second-hand. The Robert Walters 2026 AI engineer rates come from third-party summaries of its guide. Its Head of Data figure is from the 2025 guide. Flowtivity's range comes via Factoryjet.

Location. Re:Sourced puts Melbourne 5–8% below Sydney and Brisbane 10–15% below. National figures (Paymetric, Clicks) likely understate Sydney slightly.

Access. This session's network policy blocked direct page access, so every figure was read from search-engine extracts of the source page. We did not fetch AusTender data ourselves.

International context. UK ITJobsWatch data shows the same split. AI consultant contracts advertise a £563 median (72 rates, six months to August 2026), far below UK consultancy price guides.
